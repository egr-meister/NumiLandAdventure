# Privacy Policy — NumiLand Adventure

**Last updated: 2026**

NumiLand Adventure is a math learning app for children.

## We do not collect personal data
NumiLand Adventure does **not** collect, store, or share any personal
information from children or any other users.

## No accounts, no tracking
- No sign-up or login is required.
- No analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers are used.
- No data is transmitted to any server. The app works fully offline.

## Local data only
Your learning progress and settings are stored **only on your device** using
local storage. This data never leaves the device and can be erased at any time
using the **Reset Progress** button inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

## No ads and no purchases
The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no external links
inside the children's area.

## Children's privacy
This app is designed to be safe for children. It complies with child-safety
principles by collecting no personal data and including no gambling-style
mechanics.

## Contact
For questions about this policy, please contact the app developer through the
distribution platform listing (e.g., Google Play).
